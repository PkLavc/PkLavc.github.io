Introducing Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar

Google DeepMind has launched Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar, bringing real-time visual presence to conversational AI through low-latency streaming video and dynamic visual personas.

Published September 24, 20268 min readAI and models

Confirmed facts

Google DeepMind has launched Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar, a new feature designed to bring real-time visual presence to Gemini's conversational AI.

This system natively couples live dialogue capabilities with low-latency streaming video, creating an experience where the AI listens, sees, and speaks with a dynamic visual persona.

The Live Avatar feature includes precise lip-syncing, natural expressions, and fluid turn-taking.

It supports native multilingual speech-to-speech synchronization, adapting lip-sync and expressions seamlessly across 97 languages.

Live Avatar processes visual and audio inputs simultaneously to generate enriching conversations.

Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar became generally available in Gemini Enterprise on September 24, 2026, with endpoints available in the US and EU.

This release builds on the momentum of the Gemini 3.8 Live launch from the previous week.

All AI-generated output from this system is watermarked with SynthID to ensure detectability and help minimize misinformation and misattribution.

Custom avatar creation is subject to a strict enterprise allowlisting and verification process.

Developers can utilize the Google Agent Development Kit (ADK) for memory definition and direct audio streaming.

Asynchronous tool calling allows background task execution during conversations without interrupting the conversational flow.

Gemini 3.8 Live accepts audio, images, video, and text inputs with a context window of up to 128K tokens.

With Live Avatar, the system outputs audio, video, and text, subject to a 24K-token output limit.

Technical analysis

The introduction of Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar represents a significant technical leap in human-computer interaction, moving beyond traditional text or audio-only conversational AI to incorporate a real-time visual dimension. This advancement addresses the long-standing challenge of creating more natural and intuitive AI conversational experiences, particularly for enterprise applications. The core innovation lies in the native coupling of live dialogue capabilities with low-latency streaming video, which allows the AI to manifest a dynamic visual persona. This integration aims to enhance user engagement and understanding by providing visual cues that are inherent to human communication, such as facial expressions and lip movements. The system's general availability in Gemini Enterprise, along with US and EU endpoints, signals its readiness for production-grade deployment in various business contexts.

The operational paradigm of Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar is centered on simultaneous multimodal processing. The AI system is engineered to process visual and audio inputs concurrently, enabling it to 'see' and 'hear' the user in real-time. This simultaneous input processing is crucial for generating contextually relevant and responsive visual and auditory outputs. For instance, the system can interpret a user's visual cues from a live camera feed or screen share while also processing their spoken words. The output involves generating a dynamic visual persona that exhibits precise lip-syncing, natural expressions, and fluid turn-taking, all delivered via low-latency streaming video. This real-time synthesis of visual and auditory elements is fundamental to creating an immersive and believable conversational experience, where the AI's responses feel immediate and integrated.

Several core technologies underpin the capabilities of Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar. The generation of real-time visual personas with precise lip-syncing and natural expressions likely involves sophisticated generative adversarial networks (GANs) or diffusion models trained on vast datasets of human speech and corresponding facial movements. The ability to adapt lip-sync and expressions seamlessly across 97 languages implies a highly generalized and robust speech-to-face generation model that can handle diverse phonetics and linguistic nuances without visual degradation or 'drift'. Low-latency streaming video is critical for maintaining the 'live' aspect of the interaction, requiring optimized video encoding, transmission, and decoding pipelines to minimize delays. Furthermore, the multimodal input processing, which allows the AI to interpret both visual and audio streams simultaneously, points to advanced neural architectures capable of fusing information from different modalities to build a comprehensive understanding of the conversational context.

This new offering marks a significant evolution from previous generations of conversational AI. While earlier systems primarily focused on natural language understanding and generation, often relying on text-to-speech for auditory output, Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar introduces a native visual presence. This is not merely a bolted-on avatar layer but an integrated system where the visual persona is dynamically generated in conjunction with the live dialogue. The native speech-to-speech foundation of Gemini 3.8 Live, which enables fluid and responsive dialogue, is now augmented by real-time video generation. This integration allows for more natural interruption recovery and maintains conversational context, even during complex backend transactions. The shift towards a natively coupled multimodal experience fundamentally changes the interaction paradigm, making AI agents more akin to human interlocutors.

For software engineers and enterprises, Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar introduces several practical engineering consequences and opportunities. Its general availability in Gemini Enterprise means that developers can now integrate these advanced multimodal capabilities into their applications with enterprise-grade compliance, strict data governance, and provisioned throughput. The Google Agent Development Kit (ADK) plays a crucial role by providing tools for memory definition and direct audio streaming, simplifying the development of sophisticated AI agents. ADK allows agents to retain information, learn from past interactions, and maintain continuity within and across conversations, addressing the stateless nature of underlying large language models. The inclusion of asynchronous tool calling is particularly impactful, enabling agents to execute complex background tasks, such as API calls or data retrieval, without interrupting the ongoing conversation. This capability allows for more efficient and seamless user experiences, as the AI can acknowledge requests and continue dialogue while tasks are being completed in the background.

The capabilities of Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar open doors for a wide array of enterprise applications. In customer service, visually present AI agents can provide more engaging and empathetic interactions, potentially improving customer satisfaction. For interactive training and education, virtual instructors with dynamic visual personas can deliver more immersive and personalized learning experiences. Virtual assistants can become more intuitive and helpful by leveraging visual cues from users and responding with expressive video. Examples include an insurance claims agent processing visual evidence of damage while conversing with a client, or a hotel check-in agent handling guest details in the background without conversational pauses. The ability to process live camera feeds and screen shares alongside audio further expands its utility in scenarios requiring visual understanding.

Responsible AI development is a key consideration, and Google DeepMind has integrated safeguards into Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar. All AI-generated output, including audio and video streams, is watermarked with SynthID. SynthID embeds imperceptible digital watermarks directly into AI-generated content, making it detectable by specialized technology. This helps to ensure transparency, minimize misinformation, and prevent misattribution of AI-generated content. Furthermore, the creation of custom avatars is subject to a strict enterprise allowlisting and verification process. This control mechanism is designed to safeguard identity and prevent misuse, addressing potential ethical concerns related to the generation of realistic visual personas.
