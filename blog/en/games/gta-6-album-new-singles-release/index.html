CONFIRMED: Grand Theft Auto VI Album Receives Two New Singles from Cardi B and Fuerza Regida

Rockstar Games confirms the release of two new singles, 'Don't Chart' by Cardi B and 'Suzuki' by Fuerza Regida, for the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI official soundtrack album, offering a fresh listen to the game's musical direction.

Published October 9, 20261 min readMusic
Image source: Rockstar Games

Confirmed facts

PkLavc Games can confirm that Rockstar Games, in partnership with Atlantic Records, has officially released two new singles for the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI album. The newly available tracks are 'Don't Chart' by Grammy-winning artist Cardi B and 'Suzuki' by the acclaimed regional Mexican group Fuerza Regida. These releases arrive just hours after Rockstar Games provided initial glimpses of various in-game radio stations and unveiled their respective hosts, setting the stage for the game's expansive audio experience [1]. The official Grand Theft Auto VI album, which was first announced last month, is confirmed to feature a substantial collection of 34 original tracks. The complete album is slated for a global launch on November 19, 2026, aligning with the release date of Grand Theft Auto VI itself [1].

Technical analysis

The introduction of these new singles offers further insight into the aesthetic and potential in-game radio station content of Grand Theft Auto VI. This expands the officially revealed soundtrack, providing a clearer picture of the game's musical direction. While these music releases do not directly impact core gameplay mechanics, they contribute to building anticipation and enriching the game's cultural context and immersive environment [1].
