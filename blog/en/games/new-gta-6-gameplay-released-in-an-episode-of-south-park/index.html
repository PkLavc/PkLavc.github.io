Reported GTA 6 Gameplay Debut

According to a report by RockstarINTEL, new gameplay footage for Grand Theft Auto 6 was reportedly unveiled during a recent episode of the animated television series, South Park. The publisher states that this alleged reveal took place on Wednesday night, within the 29th season of the show, which has been retitled "South America" for its current run (RockstarINTEL).

Reported South Park Integration Details

RockstarINTEL highlights that the current season of South Park has consistently incorporated jokes and references pertaining to GTA 6. The publisher further claims that the specific episode in question escalated this trend by featuring what RockstarINTEL describes as "interesting official gameplay given out by Rockstar," suggesting a direct provision of game assets or footage by the developer for the show's segment (RockstarINTEL).
