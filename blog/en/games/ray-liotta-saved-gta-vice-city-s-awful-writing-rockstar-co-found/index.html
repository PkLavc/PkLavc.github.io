Ray Liotta saved GTA: Vice City's "awful" writing, Rockstar co-founder Dan Houser says

GamesRadar+ News reports on Ray Liotta saved GTA: Vice City's "awful" writing, Rockstar co-founder Dan Houser says. "He brought a level of authenticity to Tommy"

Published October 19, 20261 min readGTA & Rockstar
Image source: GamesRadar+

Confirmed facts

Rockstar Games co-founder Dan Houser has stated that actor Ray Liotta's performance was crucial in elevating the writing of Grand Theft Auto: Vice City. According to a report by GamesRadar+ News on October 17, 2026, Houser described the game's original writing as "awful." He credited Liotta, who voiced protagonist Tommy Vercetti, with bringing "a level of authenticity to Tommy" that significantly improved the final product. This insight offers a rare look into the internal perceptions of one of Rockstar's most iconic titles.

Technical analysis

This revelation from a key figure in Rockstar's history provides valuable industry context regarding the development of classic games. It underscores the profound impact that strong voice acting can have on a game's narrative and character perception, even when the underlying script is viewed critically by its creators. The report from GamesRadar+ News highlights how a performer's talent can transcend initial creative challenges, shaping player experience and the enduring legacy of a title.
