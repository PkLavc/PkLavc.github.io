Confirmed facts

Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to launch on November 19, as reported by RockstarINTEL, placing its release less than a month away. A recent study conducted by Uswitch, and detailed by RockstarINTEL, estimates that a substantial number of employees will take sick leave on the game's release day. The Uswitch research specifically sampled 1000 British employees who expressed an affinity for video games to arrive at its projections. RockstarINTEL also highlights the international scope of the anticipation surrounding GTA 6's upcoming debut.

Technical analysis

The Uswitch study, as reported by RockstarINTEL, offers a unique perspective on the potential societal impact of a major video game launch. By surveying a targeted demographic of British employees with an interest in gaming, the study provides data points on anticipated workforce attendance. This analysis contributes to understanding the broader industry context, illustrating how highly anticipated titles like GTA 6 can influence consumer behavior beyond direct sales, potentially affecting productivity on a global scale, despite the localized nature of the initial survey data.
