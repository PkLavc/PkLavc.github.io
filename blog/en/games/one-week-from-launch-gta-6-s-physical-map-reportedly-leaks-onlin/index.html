Rumor: One week from launch, GTA 6's physical map reportedly leaks online thanks to the £350 collector's edition on show in a UK department store

With less than a week to go before GTA 6's hugely anticipated release , the game's final map - or at least a stylised poster version of it, depicting the state of Leonida's key landmarks - has surfaced online. It was reportedly snapped during Selfridge's sizable GTA 6 's takeover, and is seemingly the physical map included in Rockstar's £350 collector's edition. Read more

Collector's edition map surfaces from UK retail display

Eurogamer reports that a physical map from GTA 6's £350 collector's edition has been photographed and circulated online after being spotted at Selfridges' GTA 6 takeover event in a UK department store. The outlet states the image depicts a stylised poster version of the game's final map, showcasing the state of Leonida's key landmarks, and was reportedly snapped during the promotional display less than a week before the game's anticipated release.

The leak appears to originate from the premium collector's edition's physical contents, which include a paper map among other items. Eurogamer notes that the map's emergence aligns with retail preparations for launch, as the department store's sizable takeover suggests Rockstar's marketing partners are readying physical merchandise for the release window.

Map content and collector's edition context

According to Eurogamer, the leaked map highlights major landmarks across Leonida, offering the most detailed glimpse yet at the game's geography ahead of launch. The publication describes the map as a stylised representation rather than a precise in-game cartographic render, cautioning that it may serve promotional purposes rather than reflect exact gameplay boundaries.

Eurogamer's coverage connects the leak to the game's record-breaking preorder performance, previously reported as the most preordered title in PlayStation Store history. The £350 collector's edition, which includes the physical map, is positioned as the highest-tier retail offering, and its early appearance in a major department store indicates Rockstar's physical distribution chain is active ahead of the street date.
