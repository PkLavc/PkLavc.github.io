Game Director's GTA 6 Comment Amidst Industry News

Video Games Chronicle (VGC) reports that a game director, whose identity is not specified in the provided summary, made a casual remark concerning Grand Theft Auto 6. This comment emerged alongside news regarding the status of the Mass Effect TV series, which VGC indicates is reportedly unaffected by this week's game cancellation, and the closure of BioWare's studio. The remark suggests an anticipation for the upcoming Rockstar Games title within the professional gaming community.

While the comment from the game director does not provide any specific details about Grand Theft Auto 6's development, release date, or features, it serves as an indirect acknowledgment of the game's highly anticipated status.
