GTA 6 Vehicle Interaction Details

According to a recent report by Game Rant, an upcoming guide details how players will interact with vehicles in Grand Theft Auto 6. The guide reportedly covers methods for acquiring vehicles, including stealing them, as well as aspects of vehicle maintenance and operation (Game Rant). Game Rant's reporting suggests that players will need to understand how to "care for" their vehicles, implying potential mechanics related to durability, repair, or customization within the game. This information, if accurate, points to a detailed vehicle system.

Reported Vehicle Types and Mechanics

Game Rant's reported guide specifically mentions a "variety of vehicles," encompassing both cars and aircrafts, which players will be able to drive (Game Rant). The inclusion of aircrafts suggests diverse gameplay opportunities and potentially complex control schemes, as seen in previous Grand Theft Auto titles. The guide's focus on "driving tips" for these varied vehicle types, as reported by Game Rant, indicates that mastering different vehicle categories will be a significant part of the GTA 6 experience.
