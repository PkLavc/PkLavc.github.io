First GTA 6 TV Commercial Strips Out Steamy Shower Scenes, but Shows More of Lucia's Fancy Hair Tech

GTA 6 has its first TV commercial, and it's a little different than the launch trailer.

Published October 22, 20261 min readGTA & Rockstar
Image source: IGN

Confirmed facts

Rockstar Games has released the first television commercial for Grand Theft Auto 6. According to IGN, this new commercial presents a different edit compared to the game's initial launch trailer.

The TV spot reportedly omits certain "steamy shower scenes" that were present in the original trailer, as detailed by IGN. Conversely, the commercial highlights "more of Lucia's fancy hair tech," showcasing graphical advancements, according to IGN's report.

IGN also notes that the advertisement does not include any mention of Xbox platforms.

Technical analysis

The alteration of content for a TV commercial, as reported by IGN, suggests a strategic adaptation for broadcast standards and a broader, potentially more diverse audience than initial online trailer viewers. Removing explicit scenes while emphasizing character model fidelity, such as Lucia's hair technology, aligns with showcasing technical prowess in a general audience context.

The absence of an Xbox mention, according to IGN, could indicate a focus on specific marketing channels or a general console message without platform-specific callouts in this particular ad iteration. This approach allows Rockstar to control the narrative and highlight specific visual achievements for a mass market.
