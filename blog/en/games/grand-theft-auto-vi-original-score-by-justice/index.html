Confirmed facts

Rockstar Games has officially announced that the acclaimed French electronic music duo Justice will be responsible for the original score of Grand Theft Auto VI. This confirmation comes directly from the Rockstar Games Newswire, published on October 29, 2026 (Rockstar Games, 2026). This marks a significant detail regarding the highly anticipated title's audio production, confirming the involvement of a prominent musical act in shaping the game's sonic identity.
