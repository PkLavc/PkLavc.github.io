Alleged GTA 6 Soundtrack Details Emerge

A purported 34-song tracklist for 'GTA 6: The Album' has reportedly surfaced online, just four days before the game's anticipated release on Xbox Series X/S and PS5, according to Eurogamer (https://www.eurogamer.net/gta-6-tracklist-leak). The leak, as reported by Eurogamer, stems from a video that appears to show vinyl records of the soundtrack, allegedly received early by a record store. This incident adds to a series of leaks that have reportedly affected the highly anticipated title over the past year, as noted by the publisher.

Reported Artist Inclusions

The leaked tracklist reportedly features prominent artists such as Dua Lipa, Lana Del Rey, and Rina Sawayama, as detailed by Eurogamer (https://www.eurogamer.net/gta-6-tracklist-leak). The full list of 34 songs was allegedly displayed in the video, providing a potential glimpse into the musical landscape of the upcoming game. The proximity to the launch date has not, according to Eurogamer, stemmed the tide of leaks surrounding the title.
