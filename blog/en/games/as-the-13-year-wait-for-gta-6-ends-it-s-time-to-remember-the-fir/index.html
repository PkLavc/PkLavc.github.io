As the 13-year wait for GTA 6 ends, it’s time to remember the first 10 games released in 12 years

The wait for GTA 6 is even more astounding when you remember how things used to be

Published November 15, 20261 min readGTA & Rockstar
Image source: GamesRadar+

Confirmed facts

The anticipation for Grand Theft Auto 6 has now spanned approximately 13 years, marking a significant period between mainline entries. This extended development cycle stands in stark contrast to Rockstar Games' historical output for the series, according to a report from GamesRadar+ News. The publisher previously released the first ten Grand Theft Auto titles within a concentrated 12-year timeframe, as detailed by the same source (Source 1). This historical pace highlights the current wait for GTA 6 as particularly remarkable.

Technical analysis

The shift from a rapid release schedule to the current extended development period for Grand Theft Auto 6 offers a contextual insight into modern AAA game production. While not detailing specific technical aspects of GTA 6, the reported change in Rockstar's release cadence, as noted by GamesRadar+ News (Source 1), suggests an evolution in development scope and ambition. This aligns with broader industry trends where flagship titles often require longer cycles due to increased graphical fidelity, open-world complexity, and online integration, reflecting a more intensive production model compared to earlier eras of gaming.
