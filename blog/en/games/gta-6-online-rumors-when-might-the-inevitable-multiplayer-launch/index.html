Rumor: GTA 6 online rumors: When might the inevitable multiplayer launch happen?

Despite no official word on the subject, we aren't buying that there's no GTA 6 online.

Published November 17, 20261 min readGTA & Rockstar
Official promotional image; not leaked footage. Image source: Rockstar Games

PC Gamer expects GTA 6 to eventually get an online mode

PC Gamer published a piece on November 16, 2026 arguing that a GTA 6 online component is likely, stating that despite no official word on the subject, the outlet "isn't buying that there's no GTA 6 online." According to PC Gamer, the question is not whether Rockstar will pursue multiplayer for GTA 6, but when such a launch might happen, with the article framing the eventual arrival of online play as all but inevitable.

The report is an outlet-level argument rather than a developer statement: PC Gamer offers no announced date, feature set, or business model, and attributes its expectation to the publisher's track record rather than to any Rockstar or Take-Two communication.

What players can actually rely on right now

Nothing in the supplied reporting comes from a primary Rockstar Games or Take-Two source, so there is no verified launch window, platform plan, pricing, or gameplay detail to act on. Any timeline for GTA 6 online play remains PC Gamer's interpretation, and players should treat specific dates or feature claims circulating elsewhere as speculation until Rockstar publishes its own announcement.

Practically, the only confirmed position is the absence of one: Rockstar has not publicly detailed an online mode for GTA 6, so there are no sign-up steps, beta windows, or prerequisites to follow. When an official statement arrives, it will supersede this kind of secondary coverage.

  • Source: PC Gamer opinion piece, published 2026-11-16, tier B secondary reporting
  • No Rockstar or Take-Two announcement, date, or feature list exists in the evidence
  • Treat all timing claims as the outlet's expectation, not developer confirmation
