GTA 6: Create Your Free Vice City Boarding Pass with Your Name

Your ticket to Vice City is ready. A free fan-made tool from Portal Viciados lets GTA 6 fans create a personalized FlyUS boarding pass, complete with a passenger name, seat, flight number and QR code.

Published October 18, 20262 min readGTA & Rockstar
Example ticket made with the free Portal Viciados generator. Passenger: PkLavc. Portal Viciados

How to create your own Vice City boarding pass

  1. Open the free generator on Portal Viciados.
  2. Enter a nickname or first name and, optionally, a departure city.
  3. Choose Economy, Business or First Class and select the ticket language.
  4. Generate your FlyUS ticket, then download it or share it in a story or post.

Create your free Vice City boarding pass ↗

What is included in the ticket?

The fan-made ticket includes a unique passenger number, seat and flight reference, along with a QR code pointing back to its own page. There are three visual classes and downloadable formats for regular posts and vertical stories. It is free and requires no account.
