One day before launch, ARPG Emberville gets delayed until 2027

Don't blame this one on GTA 6: The developers say incorporating feedback on Emberville's demo is taking longer than they expected.

Published October 27, 20261 min readGTA & Rockstar
Image source: PC Gamer

Confirmed facts

The action RPG Emberville has been delayed until 2027, according to a report by PC Gamer (Source 1). This postponement was announced just one day before the game's anticipated launch. PC Gamer states that the developers cited the need for additional time to integrate feedback gathered from Emberville's demo as the primary reason for the delay. Notably, the developers explicitly clarified that the delay is not attributable to Grand Theft Auto 6 (GTA 6), as reported by PC Gamer (Source 1).

Technical analysis

The developer's specific mention of GTA 6, as highlighted in PC Gamer's report (Source 1), offers a glimpse into the broader industry's perception of Rockstar Games' highly anticipated title. This detail suggests an awareness among game developers regarding the potential for major releases like GTA 6 to be speculated as influencing the development timelines of other projects. According to PC Gamer (Source 1), this direct clarification serves to manage public expectations and preemptively address any assumptions about GTA 6's impact on Emberville's production schedule.
