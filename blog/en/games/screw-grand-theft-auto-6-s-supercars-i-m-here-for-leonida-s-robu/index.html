Screw Grand Theft Auto 6's supercars, I'm here for Leonida's robust public transportation system

PC Gamer reports on Screw Grand Theft Auto 6's supercars, I'm here for Leonida's robust public transportation system. Apparently they turn up on time and everything.

Published October 30, 20261 min readGTA & Rockstar
Image source: PC Gamer

Confirmed facts

According to a recent report by PC Gamer, Grand Theft Auto 6 will feature a public transportation system within its setting, Leonida. The publication highlights that this in-game system is notably "robust" and, crucially, its services are reported to "turn up on time". This observation suggests a level of operational reliability for public transit within the upcoming title, potentially offering players consistent travel options.

Technical analysis

PC Gamer's reporting provides valuable game industry context regarding anticipated features in Grand Theft Auto 6. The emphasis on the punctuality and robustness of Leonida's public transportation system, as noted by the source, indicates a potential design choice by Rockstar Games to enhance the realism and dynamism of the game world. This detail, while seemingly minor, could significantly contribute to player immersion and offer a practical alternative to private vehicle use for navigating the game's expansive environment, aligning with a more detailed and interactive open-world design philosophy.
