Rumor: US government leaks the best GTA 6 promo yet: Pißwasser is real

Rockstar appears to be teaming up with a Florida brewery to bring two of GTA's most famous beer brands to life.

Published October 21, 20261 min readGTA & Rockstar
Official promotional image; not leaked footage. Image source: Rockstar Games

GTA 6 Promotional Beer Brands Revealed

According to PC Gamer, a US government leak from the Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau (TTB) suggests Rockstar Games is partnering with a Florida brewery to launch real-world versions of two iconic Grand Theft Auto beer brands. The report indicates that Pißwasser and Logger Light are set to become tangible products, potentially serving as a major promotional tie-in for the upcoming GTA 6.

Details from the TTB Filing

PC Gamer reports that the TTB filing lists "Pißwasser Lager" and "Logger Light Lager," with Rockstar Games identified as the applicant. The filing reportedly includes images of the distinctive in-game logos for both brands. The Florida-based brewery involved in this apparent collaboration is identified as Brew Hub, located in St. Petersburg, according to the same source. This suggests a direct involvement from Rockstar in bringing these fictional beverages to market.
