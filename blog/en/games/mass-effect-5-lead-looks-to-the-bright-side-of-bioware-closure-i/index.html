Confirmed facts

According to GamesRadar+ News, a lead developer for the now-closed Mass Effect 5 project made a notable comment regarding the upcoming Grand Theft Auto 6. Following the closure of BioWare's Mass Effect 5 development, the developer reportedly stated, "I don't have to call in sick when GTA 6 comes out." This remark was shared as part of a broader discussion about the outpouring of support received after the studio's closure, as reported on October 23, 2026.

Technical analysis

This statement, reported by GamesRadar+ News, provides insight into the significant anticipation surrounding Grand Theft Auto 6 within the video game industry itself. While a lighthearted observation made in a challenging personal context, it underscores the widespread cultural impact and perceived importance of Rockstar Games' next major title. The comment reflects a common sentiment among players and developers alike, highlighting GTA 6's status as one of the most eagerly awaited releases, even among those directly involved in game development.
