Nearly 1 in 3 gamers plan to buy GTA 6 in US, analyst says: "A rare cultural and commercial moment"

"Players have been waiting 13 years for a sequel to Grand Theft Auto 5"

Published October 28, 20261 min readGTA & Rockstar
Image source: GamesRadar+

Confirmed facts

Nearly one in three gamers in the United States intend to purchase Grand Theft Auto 6, according to analyst Michael Pachter of Wedbush Securities (GamesRadar+ News). Pachter described this level of anticipation as "a rare cultural and commercial moment" for the gaming industry. This strong consumer interest follows a 13-year wait for a sequel to Grand Theft Auto 5 (GamesRadar+ News).

Technical analysis

The reported high purchase intent highlights the significant market impact and cultural relevance of the Grand Theft Auto franchise (GamesRadar+ News). Such widespread consumer commitment before release underscores the potential for record-breaking sales and sustained engagement, reflecting the long-term anticipation built since the previous installment. This data provides valuable industry context regarding player expectations and the commercial viability of highly anticipated titles (GamesRadar+ News).
