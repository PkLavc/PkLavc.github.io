Save $10 on a Gaming Gift Card When You Buy a Select Game, Including GTA 6

Best Buy is running a promotion that saves you $10 off a gift card to PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, or Nintendo eShop, when you buy one of the biggest games of the year.

Published October 20, 20261 min readGTA & Rockstar
Image source: IGN

Confirmed facts

Best Buy is currently running a promotional offer where customers can save $10 on a gaming gift card when purchasing a select game, according to IGN. The eligible gift cards include those for the PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, or Nintendo eShop. Grand Theft Auto VI is explicitly listed among the 'biggest games of the year' that qualify for this promotion, as reported by IGN.

Technical analysis

This promotion, reported by IGN, highlights GTA 6's anticipated market position as a major title, even prior to its release, by including it in a significant retail offer. While not a direct announcement from Rockstar Games or Take-Two, the inclusion of GTA 6 in such a broad-reaching consumer incentive by a major retailer like Best Buy provides industry context regarding its expected commercial impact. There are no technical implications for the game's development or features from this retail promotion.
