Privacy Policy

This page explains how pklavc.com handles basic technical data, cookies, advertising technology, analytics signals, and direct contact information.

Cookies and advertising

pklavc.com may use cookies or similar browser technologies to keep the site working, understand aggregated traffic, improve pages, and support advertising on eligible blog articles.

If advertising is enabled, Google may act as an ad vendor. Google and its partners may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on visits to this site or other websites. Depending on region, consent, browser settings, and Google account settings, these ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Visitors can manage personalized advertising preferences through Google My Ad Center. Browser settings may also allow users to block or delete cookies.

Technical data and analytics

The site may process basic technical information such as page URLs, referrers, browser type, device information, approximate location derived from network data, timestamps, and error or access logs. This information is used to maintain reliability, understand aggregated usage, and detect abuse or operational issues.

The visitor map and related metrics are intended to be aggregated and anonymous. They are used to show general access patterns, not to identify individual visitors.

Contact information

When a visitor sends an email or uses a contact link, the provided message, email address, and related communication metadata may be used to respond to the request and keep a professional record of the conversation.

External links

Articles and project pages may link to external documentation, repositories, services, or references. Those websites have their own privacy practices and are not controlled by pklavc.com.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the site changes, when advertising configuration changes, or when new technical services are added. The latest version will remain available at this URL.
